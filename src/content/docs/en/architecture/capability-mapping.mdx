Decernia applies a structured approach to map organization-wide capabilities and domain boundaries. These instruments are designed to seamlessly align strategic objectives with underlying process design and IT architecture. Below is a curated overview of our guides and standards for capability mapping within complex ecosystems.

### Architecture Domains & Capabilities

**Business Functions & Capability Models**

- Detailed specifications and practical frameworks for developing an enterprise capability map
- Visual guides for identifying core capabilities and driving strategic transformations

**Domain Architecture Handbook**

- Instructions for scoping and structuring information and application domains
- Strategies for managing cross-domain dependencies and information flows

**Ecosystem & Federation Catalog**

- Exploration of various components and agreement frameworks within complex multi-partner collaborations
- Guidelines for selecting the appropriate architectural building blocks for secure data exchange

**ArchiMate Modeling Guidelines**

- Comprehensive procedures for modeling capabilities, processes, and applications in ArchiMate
- Relationship specifications, viewpoints, and best practices for consistent visual representations
