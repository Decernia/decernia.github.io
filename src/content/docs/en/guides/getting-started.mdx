## Setup Instructions

<Steps>

1. Initial Inspection & Access
   Verify your access credentials and review the provided repository and workspace structure. Familiarize yourself with the core architecture components.

2. Safety & Governance Checks
   Review the security and compliance documentation before configuring your environment. Ensure proper data protection protocols and policies are active.

3. Environment Calibration
   Follow the setup guidelines to calibrate your toolchain and integration settings. Confirm that database connections and authentication endpoints are functioning properly.

</Steps>

## Maintenance & Best Practices

<Aside type="tip">
  Always archive older configuration snapshots before making structural changes.
  Maintain clear versioning across all architectural models.
</Aside>

Regular maintenance ensures structural stability and long-term clarity. Audit your models regularly, review dependency graphs, and keep documentation synchronized with implementation changes.
