## Approach & Onboarding Steps

<Steps>

1. **Problem Scoping & Context**  
   Map out the organizational and ecosystem-wide context clearly. Identify key stakeholders, dependencies, and interests across the domain.

2. **Framework Alignment & Scenario Analysis**  
   Determine applicable standards (such as GDPR, NEN 7510 / ISO 27001, or reference architectures) and formulate well-substantiated scenarios to support decision-making.

3. **Design & Governance Embedment**  
   Document agreed parameters into a structured PSA or target state architecture, ensuring proper governance frameworks and ArchiMate models to anchor long-term implementation.

</Steps>

## Quality & Maintenance

<Aside type="tip">
  Keep architectural frameworks actionable by periodically reviewing and
  auditing them against evolving regulatory requirements and operational
  realities.
</Aside>

Sustainable system architecture requires continuous alignment. Ensure clear handovers and feedback loops between executives, enterprise architects, and implementation teams to maintain quality and project predictability.
